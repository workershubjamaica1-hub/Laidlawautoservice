How AutoAid 24/7 fits in (everyone)

  • AutoAid 24/7 is a platform that brings customers together with independent technicians, tow operators, parts vendors, accessories sellers and rental companies. We are not the mechanic, the tow operator, the seller or the rental company, and we do not employ them. The work, the sale or the rental is between the customer and the provider.
  • Providers are independent businesses. They decide how they do their work, they are responsible for their own tools, vehicles, insurance, licences, permits and taxes, and they are not our employees, partners or agents.
  • Our fees are shown in the app before they apply: the job fee for repairs, roadside assistance and towing, the commission on parts and accessories, and the commission on rentals. We may change a fee going forward and will show the new rate in the app.
  • You must give true information and keep your account safe. You must not take a customer, a job, a sale or a rental that was introduced through AutoAid 24/7 away from the platform to avoid a fee.
  • Ratings and reviews must be honest. We may remove one that is false, abusive or about something else.
  • We may suspend or close an account for false information, unsafe behaviour, repeated complaints, abuse, fraud or unpaid fees. Where we can, we will tell you why.

Customer Agreement

This applies to every customer who asks for a repair, roadside assistance, towing, parts, accessories (with or without fitting) or a rental.

  • Authority over the vehicle. You confirm that the vehicle is yours, or that you have the owner's permission to have work done on it, to have it towed, and to have parts or accessories fitted to it.
  • Accurate requests. Describe the problem and your location as accurately as you can. Emergency requests are for real emergencies. A false or repeated false request can lead to your account being closed.
  • Your safety. In an emergency, put your safety first: move to a safe place if you can, switch on your hazard lights, and call the emergency services if anyone is hurt or in danger. AutoAid 24/7 is not an emergency service and cannot promise a response time. The arrival time is the one the technician gives you in the chat.
  • Prices. The technician sends you a price and an arrival time in the chat. Nothing starts until you agree. A "typical price" shown in the app is only a guide. If the work turns out to need more than was agreed, the technician must tell you and get your agreement before going further.
  • Paying. You pay in the way shown on the job: in person to the provider, or online through the payment link (when it is switched on), in which case AutoAid 24/7 holds the payment until you confirm the work is done, then pays the provider. Do not pay anyone outside the way the job shows.
  • Confirming the work. Check the work before you confirm it is done. If something is wrong, use "Report a problem" instead of confirming. A job that you do not answer for 24 hours is confirmed automatically unless a problem was reported.
  • Cancelling. You can cancel a job before the price is agreed at no charge. After that, tell the provider in the chat; if the provider has already travelled to you or started work, they may charge a fair amount for the time and cost already spent, which we will ask both of you to agree.
  • Towing and storage. You authorise the tow operator to move your vehicle to the place you agree. Remove valuables and personal items. Tell the operator about any damage, a locked steering, a flat tyre or anything else that makes the vehicle hard to move. The operator will note the condition of the vehicle when it is collected and delivered.
  • Roadside photos and location. If you share a photo or your location, you agree that the technician who accepts the job, and our administrators, can see it for the purpose of the job.
  • Parts and accessories. Parts and accessories are sold by independent vendors. Check what you are given against what was offered before you pay. If you ask for fitting, the fitting charge is part of the agreed price and the seller is responsible for the quality of the fitting (see the Accessories Seller and Installer Agreement).
  • Rentals. Renting a vehicle is covered by the Renter Agreement below.
  • Our limit. We do our best to keep the platform working and to help when something goes wrong, but we are not responsible for the quality of work or goods supplied by independent providers, or for delays outside our control, except where the law does not allow us to exclude that.

Service Provider Agreement (technicians and tow operators)

This applies to every technician or tow operator who applies through AutoAid 24/7, together with the schedule for each department you choose to work in.

  • Independent business. You are an independent service provider. You are responsible for your skills, tools, vehicles, insurance, licences, permits, taxes, safety equipment and the safety of anyone working with you.
  • True information. What you tell us about your skills, experience, business and the departments you work in must be true. Only choose departments you are properly able to do. Emergency work (roadside assistance and towing) is given only to providers whose identity AutoAid 24/7 has checked, and we may ask for ID, proof of experience or proof of insurance and licences at any time.
  • Going online. When you switch Online you tell us you are free to take jobs now. Only accept a job you can really do. If you accept, tell the customer an honest price and arrival time, and keep to it. If you cannot, tell the customer in the chat straight away and step back from the job.
  • Prices and extra work. Do not start before the customer agrees the price. Do not add work or charges that the customer has not agreed. Give honest advice. Do not recommend work that is not needed.
  • Standard of work. Do the work with proper care, skill and safety, using parts that are as described. Fix faults in your own work at no extra charge when the customer tells you of them within a reasonable time, or put them right some other way you agree with the customer.
  • Customer vehicle and property. Treat the customer's vehicle and belongings with care. You are responsible for damage you cause. Note the condition of the vehicle (and take photos where it helps) before you start, especially for towing and body work.
  • Fees and payment. AutoAid 24/7 takes the job fee shown in the app (a percentage of the agreed price). For jobs paid in person, you collect the full price and the fee shows as owed by you to AutoAid 24/7, which you must settle when asked. For jobs paid online, we pay you the price less the fee after the customer confirms. Provider subscriptions, when switched on, must be kept up to date to receive jobs.
  • Customer details. Use a customer's name, phone number, location and photos only to do the job. Do not share them, keep them after the job, or use them to contact the customer for any other reason.
  • Conduct. Be respectful and honest. No harassment, discrimination, threats, working under the influence of alcohol or drugs, or asking customers for money outside the agreed job.
  • Going around the platform. Do not take a customer you met on AutoAid 24/7 to another channel to avoid the fee for that job.
  • Complaints. If a customer reports a problem, you will answer honestly and help to resolve it. We may hold an online payment while we look into it, and we may suspend you while a serious complaint is looked into.
  • Ending. You can stop using AutoAid 24/7 at any time once your open jobs are finished and any fees you owe are paid. We may end your access under the rules in "How AutoAid 24/7 fits in".

Schedule A: Mechanical, diagnostics and maintenance

  • Give a clear written or chat description of what was found and what you will do, before you start.
  • Keep parts you replace for the customer to see if they ask, unless you agreed otherwise (for example a returned core).
  • Use parts that are as described to the customer (new, used, aftermarket or reconditioned) and tell the customer which they are.
  • Do not tell a customer a vehicle is safe to drive when it is not. If you find a serious safety fault, tell the customer plainly.
  • Test drive a customer's vehicle only if they agree, and only if you are licensed to drive it.

Schedule B: Tire

  • Fit tyres and wheels that suit the vehicle (size, load and speed rating). Tell the customer when a tyre is worn or unsafe.
  • Tighten wheel nuts to the right torque and tell the customer to have them checked after a short distance.
  • Be clear whether a tyre is new or used, and the price of balancing, valves, disposal and any other extra before you start.

Schedule C: Electrical

  • Disconnect power safely and protect the vehicle's electronics. Tell the customer if a repair could reset or affect the vehicle's computers, locks, alarm or radio code.
  • Use wiring, fuses and parts that suit the vehicle. Do not bypass safety systems (airbags, ABS, immobilisers) or leave work that is a fire risk.
  • Explain clearly when a fault is found but cannot be fixed on the spot.

Schedule D: Auto body repairs

  • Agree the scope, materials and finish (for example repair, panel replacement, repaint) in writing in the chat before you start. Record the damage that was there before you begin, with photos.
  • Say honestly how long the work will take and whether the vehicle must stay with you. You are responsible for a vehicle in your care until it is handed back.
  • Colour matching and finish are never exactly perfect on older paint. Tell the customer what to expect.
  • Do not carry out structural repairs that affect the safety of the vehicle unless you are qualified to do so. Tell the customer if a repair might need an insurance or inspection process.

Schedule E: Roadside assistance

  • Roadside jobs happen on public roads and at the customer's location. Put safety first: use warning signs or lights where needed, park safely, and do not carry out work in a place that is unsafe for you or the customer.
  • Be clear about what you can do at the roadside (for example change a tyre, jump start, bring a small amount of fuel, open a locked vehicle) and what needs a workshop or a tow.
  • Only open a locked vehicle for the person who can show it is theirs or that they have permission. Do not give or sell another person's keys, codes or entry methods.
  • If it turns out to be a tow job, tell the customer and agree the new price before you do anything else.
  • You must be reachable by phone while on an emergency job, and tell the customer and AutoAid 24/7 if you will be late.

Schedule F: Wrecker and towing

  • You must hold the licences, permits, registration, fitness and insurance that the law requires for the vehicle you use and for the work you do, and give us proof when asked. Do not take towing jobs unless these are in place.
  • Use equipment that suits the customer's vehicle and secure it properly. Tell the customer if you cannot tow it safely (for example because of its weight, a locked steering, or damage) and do not attempt it.
  • Before you move the vehicle, record its condition with photos or a note (all sides, existing damage) and agree where it is going. Record it again on delivery.
  • You are responsible for loss or damage caused while the vehicle is in your care or on your equipment, including during storage that you provide.
  • Agree the price (including distance, storage, waiting time and any extra) before you move the vehicle. Do not hold a vehicle for charges that were not agreed.
  • Do not tow a vehicle away from private property or a public road without the owner's or the authorised person's request, or without a legal order.

Parts Vendor Agreement

  • Sold by you. You sell parts as an independent business. The sale is between you and the customer. AutoAid 24/7 provides the chat and the way to agree a price.
  • Honest description. Say truthfully what the part is (brand, part number, what it fits) and whether it is new, used, aftermarket or reconditioned. Do not sell stolen, counterfeit or illegal goods. We may ask where a part came from.
  • Fit. Ask the customer for the vehicle details (the chat shows them) and offer a part that fits. If it does not fit, put it right (exchange or refund) unless you told the customer clearly that it was a no-return sale, before they agreed.
  • Your terms. Tell the customer in the chat about any warranty, return or exchange rules before they accept your offer.
  • Price and payment. The offer total is what the customer pays you, in person, when they collect the part. Do not ask for more. Mark an order as picked up and paid only when it is.
  • Commission. You owe AutoAid 24/7 the commission shown in the app on each completed order, calculated on the offer total. We keep a record of what you owe and you settle it when asked. We may pause your account if it is not paid.
  • Customer details. You see a customer's phone number only after they accept an offer. Use it only for that order.
  • Complaints. Answer a customer's complaint fairly and quickly. Repeated complaints, false descriptions or non-payment of commission can lead to suspension.

Accessories Seller and Installer Agreement

This applies to sellers of auto accessories and car cosmetics (for example lights, seat covers, window tint, rims, sound systems, body kits, wraps and detailing products), and to sellers who also fit them. It includes everything in the Parts Vendor Agreement, plus:

  • Fitting is your work. If a customer asks for fitting, you do it, or you arrange it, as an independent business and you are responsible for the quality of it. The fitting charge is shown separately in your offer, and it is part of the total price the customer pays you.
  • Where it is fitted. Fit it at your shop, or at the customer's location if you offered that. When you go to a customer, you are responsible for your own safety and equipment, and you leave the place clean and safe.
  • Check before you fit. Look at the vehicle and note its condition (with photos where useful) before you start. Tell the customer if an accessory will not fit properly, could harm the vehicle, or could void any warranty.
  • The law. Accessories and fitting must follow Jamaican law, including the rules for vehicle lighting, window tint, noise, number plates and the safe use of the vehicle on the road. Do not sell or fit anything that breaks them. If you are not sure, say so and do not fit it.
  • Electrical and safety systems. Do not interfere with airbags, brakes, steering, seat belts or other safety systems. Disconnect power safely, protect the vehicle's electronics, and tell the customer if the work could affect alarms, locks or radio codes.
  • Damage during fitting. You are responsible for damage you cause to the vehicle or its contents while it is with you or while you work on it. Put it right at your cost.
  • Workmanship and warranty. Fix faults in your fitting at no extra charge when the customer tells you of them within the time you stated in your offer (or a reasonable time if you stated none).
  • Commission. The commission on a completed accessories order is calculated on the whole agreed total, fitting included, at the rate shown in the app.
  • Marking it done. Mark an order as done and paid only when the accessory has been collected or fitted and paid for.

Rental Company Agreement

  • Your business. You rent out vehicles as an independent business. The rental is between you and the renter. AutoAid 24/7 provides the listing, the request, the chat and the dispute process.
  • The vehicles. Each vehicle you list must be yours or one you are allowed to rent out, in a safe and roadworthy condition, with valid registration and fitness, and with the insurance the law requires for hiring it out. Tell renters clearly what insurance and cover is included, and what they are responsible for (for example the excess).
  • True listing. Photos, prices, the daily rate, the deposit, the minimum days (at least 3), seats and your requirements must be accurate. Do not charge more than the listed daily rate and the agreed deposit, plus the late days and any amounts decided under this agreement.
  • Renter requirements. You may set reasonable requirements (age, licence, documents). The renter's licence photo is shown to you only after you accept a booking, for checking the renter, and you must not keep it, copy it or use it for anything else.
  • Handover and return. Note the condition of the vehicle (fuel, mileage, damage, accessories) with photos when you hand it over, and again when it comes back. Mark it handed over and returned in the app on the day.
  • Reporting the return honestly. When the vehicle comes back, you say in the app whether it came back in the same condition or with a problem. If you report a problem, you must describe it truthfully and be able to show it (for example photos from before and after). The renter then confirms or disagrees. If the two of you do not agree, a dispute is filed and we follow the process in "How disputes are handled".
  • Late returns. Each full day the vehicle is kept past the return date is charged at the daily rate. The renter agrees to this when they book. We tell you and the renter when a vehicle is overdue, and record the late days and amount at return. The amount is collected as agreed with the renter and under the card or deposit arrangement used at pick-up, and AutoAid 24/7 may help record and arrange it.
  • Deposit and damage. You may keep from the deposit only what was agreed or decided under the dispute process for real damage, loss or unpaid days, and you must return the rest promptly. Do not take more than the actual cost of the damage.
  • Commission. You owe AutoAid 24/7 the commission shown in the app, calculated on the rental total (not on the deposit), for each completed rental. Late-day charges are not part of the rental total unless we say so in the app.
  • Accidents, fines and theft. You and the renter agree how accidents, traffic fines, tolls, theft and breakdowns are handled before pick-up. Report theft and serious accidents to the police and your insurer. Tell AutoAid 24/7 as soon as you can.

Renter Agreement

This is what you agree to when you request a rental in the app, by ticking the Rental Agreement box.

  • Who may rent. You hold a valid driver's licence that is accepted in Jamaica and meet the rental company's requirements (shown on the vehicle). You give us a true photo of your licence. Only the rental company whose booking you are accepted for, and our administrators, can see it.
  • Who may drive. Only you, and any other driver the rental company has approved in writing, may drive the vehicle. Do not sub-rent it, use it for hire, racing, towing or any illegal purpose, or take it out of Jamaica.
  • Rent and deposit. The daily rate and the deposit are shown before you request the booking. The minimum rental is 3 days (a company may set more). You pay the rent and the deposit as arranged with the rental company. The deposit may be held on, or taken from, a card or paid in cash, as the company and you agree at pick-up, and it may be used for amounts that are due under this agreement. AutoAid 24/7 does not take your card details in this app.
  • Check the vehicle at pick-up. Walk around the vehicle with the rental company and note any damage, the fuel level and what is in it. Take photos. If you do not note a mark at pick-up, it is harder to show it was there before.
  • Looking after the vehicle. Use it with care and keep to the law. Return it with the fuel level and in the condition you received it, apart from normal wear. You are responsible for damage, loss, missing items, traffic fines and tolls during the rental, up to the limits and in the way set out here and in the insurance the company gives you.
  • Returning on time. Return the vehicle by the end date. Each full extra day you keep it is charged at the daily rate (for a late return the number of extra days is counted when the vehicle comes back, or day by day while it is still out). You agree that these extra days can be charged to you, using the card or deposit arrangement you gave at pick-up, and that AutoAid 24/7 will tell you in the chat when a vehicle is overdue. If you need more time, ask the rental company before the end date.
  • The return check. When you return the vehicle, the rental company records whether it came back in the same condition. You are then asked to confirm that you agree. If you do not agree, say what you disagree with. If you do not answer within 3 days of the return, it is taken as agreed.
  • If you do not agree. If the rental company reports a problem, or you do not agree with their report, a dispute is filed. You agree that AutoAid 24/7 will contact you and the rental company, ask for photos or other proof, and record a decision on what, if anything, is charged or refunded under this agreement. This is not instead of any right you have under the law.
  • Accidents and breakdowns. Call the rental company straight away. After an accident, call the police if the law requires it or if anyone is hurt, do not admit blame at the scene, and write down the other party's details. Do not leave the vehicle unlocked or unattended.
  • Cancelling. You can cancel a request, or a confirmed booking, until the vehicle is handed over. After that you may only end the rental by returning the vehicle.

How disputes are handled (everyone)

  • Try the chat first. Most problems are solved by talking in the job, order or booking chat. Keep your messages polite and say exactly what happened.
  • Reporting a problem. A customer can report a problem on a repair, roadside or towing job instead of confirming it. A rental company or a renter can report a problem with a rental return. A payment that AutoAid 24/7 is holding stays held while the problem is looked into.
  • What we do. When a dispute is filed we contact each side, ask for what we need (for example photos, receipts, the vehicle's condition before and after), and then record a decision in the chat. Our decision covers what the platform can act on: releasing or refunding money we hold, recording a late fee or a deposit amount that is due under these agreements, ratings, and account access. We do not promise a particular outcome, and we do not replace the courts.
  • Be honest, and give us time. Give true information and any proof you have. False reports or fake evidence can lead to account closure. Please give us a reasonable time to review. We aim to contact each side promptly.
  • Your legal rights. Using this process does not take away any right you have under the laws of Jamaica, including the right to go to court or to a consumer or other authority.

These agreements are governed by the laws of Jamaica. If you have a question about them, contact the AutoAid 24/7 team.
