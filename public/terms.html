3. Emergencies

If anyone is hurt or in danger, call 119 (police) or 110 (fire and ambulance) first.

The roadside assistance and wrecker buttons send your request to technicians. We do our best to get help to you quickly, but we cannot promise that a technician will accept, or how long it will take, especially at busy times or in areas with few technicians. Stay somewhere safe while you wait. You can cancel a request before the work starts.

An emergency request is sent as an urgent job to technicians who are online. As soon as one accepts, their phone number is shared with you and yours with them, so you can speak right away. They then send you a price and the time they will arrive, and the work starts only if you agree. Payment, refunds and complaints work as described in section 5.

The assistant is an AI. It gives general information about vehicle problems and is not a diagnosis or a repair guarantee. It can be wrong. Do not rely on it for safety decisions: if something could be unsafe, stop driving and have a qualified technician check your vehicle.
