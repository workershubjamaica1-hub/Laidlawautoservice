Privacy Notice

Last updated: 24 October 2026

This notice explains what personal information AutoAid 24/7 collects, why, who can see it, and the choices you have. It applies to customers and to service providers who use our website.

1. Who we are

AutoAid 24/7 is a vehicle servicing and roadside assistance service in Jamaica. We connect customers with approved service providers (technicians). If you have a question about your information, contact us through the AutoAid 24/7 team.

2. What we collect

  • Your account: name, email address, phone number, address and parish. Your password is stored in a scrambled form that we cannot read.
  • Your vehicles: the make and model, year, the registration or VIN number if you add it, and a photo of your motor vehicle.
  • Your requests: service requests and what you describe, emergency requests (roadside assistance and wrecker), the status of each, the price charged, and ratings and reviews you leave.
  • Your location, only if you choose to share it: when you ask for emergency help you can tap "Share my current location". We use it to send help to you. We do not track your location at other times.
  • Mechanic chat: the messages you type to our "Ask the mechanic" assistant, and the type and year of the vehicle you pick, are sent to our AI provider to write the answer.
  • Service providers: business name, the kinds of work you do, your experience, an optional profile photo, photos of finished work, the jobs you did, what you earned and your ratings.
  • Payments: card details are typed on our payment provider's page and never reach us. We keep only the fact that a payment was made: who, how much, when and the payment reference.
  • Basic technical records such as the time of a request and error logs, so we can keep the service running and secure.

3. How we use it

  • To run your account, book services and send the nearest suitable technician (we match by parish).
  • To respond to emergency requests and let you and the technician reach each other.
  • To process payments for Premium memberships and service provider subscriptions, and to track free Premium services.
  • To show ratings and a provider's completed work, so customers can trust who they hire.
  • To keep the service safe, prevent fraud and misuse, fix problems, and follow the law.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Who can see it

  • Emergencies: a roadside or towing request shows technicians who are online your location (as you typed it, and as GPS coordinates if you shared them) and the problem. The technician who accepts also sees your phone number straight away, so you can speak.
  • Online technicians: when you book a service, technicians who are online in that department can see your vehicle, the problem and the place you gave (and your location if you shared it) before one of them accepts. The technician who accepts sees your name, and your phone number once you agree the price.
  • Technicians: approved service providers can see the details they need to do a job. For a job you book, or an emergency request you send, that includes your name, phone number, address, parish, vehicle details and photo, and the location you shared. Open emergency requests are visible to approved technicians so that one can respond quickly.
  • Rental companies: a company you ask to rent a vehicle from sees your name and what you write. Once they accept your booking they also see your phone number, parish and the photo of your driver's licence, and you see their address and phone number.
  • Parts vendors: a vendor you start a parts chat with sees your name, your vehicle (including the VIN if you added it) and what you write. After you accept their offer they also see your phone number and parish. You see their business address and phone number after you accept.
  • AutoAid 24/7 staff and administrators, to run the service and help you.
  • Companies that help us run the service: our database and photo storage provider (Supabase), our hosting provider (Render), our payment provider (Fygaro), and our AI provider (Anthropic) for the mechanic chat. They handle information only to help us run the service.
  • Authorities, if the law requires us to share information.

Customers see a technician's name, phone number, photo (if added), ratings, and the photo of the finished work for their job.

5. Photos

Photos you or a technician upload are kept in private storage. They are opened only through short-lived links, and only by the people who need them for the job.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is open and for as long as we need it for your service history, payment records and safety. If you ask us to delete your account we will do so, but we may keep records that we must keep by law or need to settle a dispute, such as payment records.

7. How we protect it

The website uses an encrypted connection, passwords are stored scrambled, photos are private, and access is limited to what each person needs. No system is perfectly secure, so please choose a strong password and keep it to yourself.

8. Your choices

  • You can update your phone, address and parish from your account.
  • You can ask us to show you the information we hold about you, correct it, or delete your account.
  • You can choose not to share your location. You can still describe where you are in words.

You may also have rights under data protection laws that apply in Jamaica. To use any of these choices, contact us using the details above.

9. Children

AutoAid 24/7 is for adults who own or drive vehicles. It is not meant for anyone under 18.

10. Changes

We may update this notice. When we make an important change we will change the date above and, where needed, ask you to agree again.

Read our Terms of Use as well.
